What the work covers

Three areas of work: day-to-day operations, marketing planning, and team recruitment.

Operations and administration

  • Administrative support

    Routine administrative tasks and organisation.

  • Document processing

    Preparation and structuring of business documentation.

  • Business communication

    Drafting and managing professional correspondence.

  • Reports and materials

    Reports, presentations and structured materials.

  • Information structuring

    Organisation of business data and processes.

  • CRM support

    Customer data management and tracking.

  • Workflow coordination

    Coordination of operational processes.

  • Compliance documentation

    Preparation of business-related documentation.

Marketing planning and execution

A marketing plan built around who you sell to and where they can be reached, prepared as documents and materials your team can put to work straight away.

  • Market and competitor research
  • Target audience and positioning
  • Channel plan and budget allocation
  • Content plan and campaign calendar
  • Campaign materials and reporting set-up

You receive: a marketing plan document, a campaign calendar and ready-to-use materials.

Team recruitment and department set-up

Recruiting a whole team rather than a single person: roles and reporting lines are documented first, then candidates are found and screened for every position.

  • Department structure map, roles and reporting lines
  • Job descriptions and a hiring plan
  • Candidate sourcing and screening at volume
  • Interview scheduling and coordination
  • Offer and onboarding paperwork

Typical teams

  • Software developers
  • Office and back-office staff
  • Customer support operators
  • Sales and account managers

Recruitment is an intermediary service. The client hires candidates directly; staff are not employed or supplied on the client's behalf, and candidates are never charged a fee.
